Road to The Counter

The Counter is rougher and more real: life skills, real work, real people. You earn your way there by showing growth. The owner reads every request and sends the invitation.

How will you show your growth?

Every way counts the same. Pick what works for you.

How will you show your growth?

Write as little or as much as you like below. Your adult helps send the rest.

1. Principles I understand

Which Whole Donuts principles do you understand now, and what did each one change for you? Use your own words.

2. Effort I kept up

What did you keep doing, for how long, and what was hard about it? Showing up counts more than being perfect.

3. What I shared

What did you pass forward: a skill, a resource, help, a kind word? Who did it help? No names needed.

4. About you

Pause before you send. Rethink it, or stop and take the night. Your road will still be here tomorrow.
