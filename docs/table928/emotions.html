EMOTIONAL TABLE
YOUR EMOTIONS, YOUR POWER
Every emotion you feel has meaning, direction, and strength. Table928 helps you understand what each emotion means, what causes it, and how to move forward with clarity and confidence.
CALM
Calm means your mind and body feel steady. It often appears after rest, safety, or understanding.
CURIOUS
Curious means your mind wants to explore or learn something new. It is the beginning of growth and discovery.
OVERWHELMED
Overwhelmed means too many thoughts or feelings at once. It signals a need for pause, space, or support.
CONFUSED
Confused means something doesn’t make sense yet. It is the first step toward clarity.
HOPEFUL
Hopeful means you can see a good future or a positive change coming. It is the spark that moves you forward.
SAD
Sad means something mattered to you and changed. It is a sign of care, connection, and healing.
ANXIOUS
Anxious means your mind is imagining danger or uncertainty. It signals a need for grounding and reassurance.
EXCITED
Excited means your energy is rising toward something good. It is the emotion of action and momentum.
WHAT TO DO NEXT
Choose an emotion above to begin your path toward healing, clarity, and direction. Each emotion leads to a deeper explanation and a simple next step.
